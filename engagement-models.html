Engagement Models

Start where it makes sense for you.

Fixed-Price Assessment

A scoped market and readiness assessment — the lowest-commitment way to start.

Best for teams still deciding whether Europe is worth the investment.

  • A scoped assessment of 2–3 priority markets.
  • Route-to-market and ICP validation grounded in your actual product.
  • A written go/no-go recommendation with a proposed next step.

Strategic Growth Retainer

Monthly, embedded execution across the Growth Framework.

Best for teams ready to commit to sustained execution.

  • Embedded, monthly execution across the full Growth Framework.
  • A dedicated point of contact who runs point on your behalf.
  • Monthly reporting against pipeline, logos and momentum.

Success-Based Incentives

Commercial terms tied to the traction actually delivered.

Best for teams that want growth de-risked commercially.

  • Commercial terms tied to qualified pipeline or closed revenue.
  • Aligned incentives — we only win when your growth does.
  • Typically layered on top of a Retainer or Fractional Leadership model.

Fractional Leadership

An outsourced commercial lead, part- or full-time.

Best for teams that need a commercial leader, not just execution.

  • An outsourced VP/Head of EMEA, part- or full-time.
  • Represents you in market — partners, customers, hires.
  • A built-in path to a permanent hire when the time is right.

Marketing & Demand-Generation

Optional add-on — available across all four models above.

Best for teams whose growth story needs a stronger pipeline engine, not just sales execution.

  • Fractional GTM/marketing leadership across positioning, messaging and channel strategy.
  • Hands-on demand-generation execution — campaigns, content, and channel testing.
  • Layered onto any of the four models above — never sold as a standalone engagement.

Common Questions

The things most teams ask before their first conversation with us.

How quickly can we start?

Most engagements begin within two to three weeks of a signed agreement — faster for a Fixed-Price Assessment, since it has the smallest scope.

Do we need to relocate anyone to Europe?

No. We are your commercial team on the ground — that is the entire point of the model. You stay focused on product and home-market growth.

What happens after the Fixed-Price Assessment?

You get a clear go/no-go recommendation and a proposed next step — typically a Retainer or Fractional Leadership engagement. There's no obligation to continue.

Can we combine models?

Yes — most long-term clients layer Success-Based Incentives or the Marketing & Demand-Generation add-on on top of a Retainer or Fractional Leadership engagement.
